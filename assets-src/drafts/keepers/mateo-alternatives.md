# Mateo art alternatives

Three full-body battle sprite drafts generated with built-in ImageGen. The original sprite supplied the face and painting style. [Original Mateo](mateo.webp)

The transparent 400 × 600 WebP drafts sit beside the original. The full-size PNGs are saved locally in `assets-src/units/samples/` under the same names.

## Shared prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Generate one new full-body battle sprite for Brinelight, a hand-painted maritime fantasy deckbuilder. The attached Mateo sprite is a character identity and painting-style reference. Preserve the recognizable adult Cuban cooper, warm brown complexion, short dark curls, neat mustache and wiry working build. Paint a new pose and costume as described below. Use the same textured naturalistic oil-painted finish, clear silhouette, worn late-19th-century harbour workwear and restrained warm lantern light. He carries a short wooden cooper's mallet in his right hand, a round shield made from a barrel lid with wood grain and iron bands on his left forearm, and a small lit brass lantern clipped at the belt. The barrel-lid shield is about torso width and has practical leather loops on the back. Both arms have the same realistic muscle mass, shoulder width and limb proportions. Let us read the left elbow and enough of the forearm to see how it sits in a leather strap behind the shield, with the same natural scale as the mallet arm. Keep the shield arm from looming toward the camera. One right hand grips the mallet handle and the left supports the shield, separated clearly. Center one figure, head to soles all visible, with air around the mallet and shield. Vertical 2:3 game sprite, three-quarter view facing slightly toward screen right, legible when small against a dark blue-green cave. Genuine alpha transparency for the entire background, no environment, cast shadow, lettering, logos, or checkerboard drawn into the image.
```
