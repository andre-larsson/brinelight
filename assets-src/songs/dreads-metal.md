# Metal songs for the Dreads and the Drowned Cantor (requests for Gemini, 2026-11-16)

The user asked for prompts "for other areas in the game, in the style of Korn/Slipknot/Mudvayne, and Iron Maiden". No
files yet. Each song is for a fight that plays only the score now: three Dreads, the Hollow Divers and the Drowned
Cantor, the one final with no song. Dark Water and the Belfry are the darker levels, so the heavy songs go there; the
Shallows keep the score.

| 2. Nobody in the Dress | Korn-like nu-metal | The Hollow Divers (Belfry) | Belfry fights with a Diver, taking turns with the score |
| 3. Bigger Bell | Mudvayne-like math metal | The Bell Yearling (Belfry Dread) | Its fight, instead of the `dread` cue |
| 4. King of the Herrings | Iron Maiden-like gallop | The Crowned Oarfish (Belfry Dread) | Its fight, instead of the `dread` cue |
| 5. Ring It Once Again | Iron Maiden-like epic with a choir | The Drowned Cantor (a Belfry final, after the Heart is home) | Its fight, instead of the `pin` cues |

Notes for the prompts:

- They describe the sound and name no band. Gemini takes an artist's name only as loose inspiration, and a description
  steers it better.
- A Dread fight often ends before a three-minute song does, so 1 to 4 ask to be heavy within the first ten seconds. The
  Cantor's fight is a long final, so its song may open slowly.
- After the Buoy refusal ("themes that might come across as potentially sensitive or dark"), the words keep the threat
  to creature-feature level: no blood, no killing, and death only as "the keepers who stayed". The style words "dark"
  and "heavy" went through in the Buoy's versions B and D.
- Each song is one voice (a male voice here, for contrast with the women's voices at the Buoy and on the title screen),
  with gang shouts or a choir where asked.

## 1. The Gardener (the Garden Strider; early-Slipknot-like, as extreme as it goes)

The Strider sings to Wren, who has been picking its beads. Its keeper's note gives the hook: it tends the garden, and it
notices what is taken. Its moves are in the breakdown (Tend the Garden, Graze, Stilt Legs). Says nothing new; the
keepers' rule "leave the garden be" is flavour.

Made more extreme twice on 2026-11-16 at the user's request: first "darker/heavier/much more intense like early
Slipknot", then "even more extreme (musicality-wise). Lyrics does not have to be very dark and extreme. only the music."
So the style now asks for everything at once (sludge to blast beats, dissonant odd-time riffs, growls and shrieks, the
kegs out of step with the kit, harsh noise), and the words are a furious groundskeeper ("Keep off!", "Put it back!")
with no threat beyond that, which also makes a refusal less likely. Earlier versions are in git history (755e390, the
melodic one; e419273, the first screamed one).

## 2. Nobody in the Dress (the Hollow Divers; Korn-like)

The pale growth inside an old diving dress sings. Its keeper's note: no keeper is in it; the growth came in through the
rotted seams, fills the canvas as a hand fills a glove and has learned to walk it; its cold lamp drinks from Wren's.
WORLD.md leaves open what became of the keepers, so the bridge asks "who wore it first?" and the growth does not know.
The threat to Wren is that her dress has seams too.

```

Something larger down here does the same." The words hint, as the note does, that the great bell is the shell it wants
("I hear one humming through the stone": the Listeners hear the Bellwether through the rock). They do not name the Thing.

```

Precise, polyrhythmic drums with many tom fills. One male voice that switches in an instant between a calm, eerie clean voice and a tight, barked yell, sometimes inside one line. Broken bronze clanging as percussion, and small bells. Tense and unpredictable. Heavy within the first ten seconds. About 3 minutes.

## 4. King of the Herrings (the Crowned Oarfish; Iron Maiden-like)

crest; this one came in from the open sea through the Belfry's broken arch; it coils round a diver and squeezes, and its
crest is sharp. The storm omen is old sailors' lore about oarfish. The agent's lore call, as a question only: it came in
toward the glow under the Belfry's floor, as everything there grew toward the Heart.

```

In the middle the tempo drops: a clean guitar and the sound of the open sea, then it builds back up into twin-guitar harmony leads and the gallop. Heroic, bright, storytelling. Starts with the twin guitars in the first seconds. About 3 minutes.

## 5. Ring It Once Again (the Drowned Cantor; Iron Maiden-like epic)

The Cantor sings, and his choir answers. It plays only after the Heart is home, so it may say more than the others. Its
keeper's note: with the Bellwether silent, the drowned keepers gather under it and try to ring it again; silence the
Cantor and the others stop and drift back into the dark. Its moves are in the words: Lead the Peal, each Toll harder
than the last, Call the Choir. From WORLD.md: the keepers once rang the bell by hand with a rope up the tower; the
Ringers went down to tend the bell and stayed. "The bell kept every name" is a hint, in the agent's lore, that the bell
keeps the keepers who stay with it; it is not a fact yet.

```
An epic early-1980s British heavy metal song with a choir. It opens slow and solemn: a great bell tolling, a low male choir humming, a clean, arpeggiated guitar, and one male tenor voice singing the first verse quietly. Then the band crashes in at a steady mid tempo, about 120 BPM, and later breaks into a fast gallop, about 165 BPM: a galloping bass, two lead guitars playing harmonised melodies, a soaring, operatic tenor. The choir answers the singer in every chorus like a congregation. In the instrumental the bell rings faster and louder on every beat. At the end the band stops, the choir fades out one voice at a time, and the bell rings once more, quietly. Grand, solemn, heroic and sad. About 3 minutes.

## When a file comes

- Level it as the other songs (GAME_NOTES.md, "Songs and the Jukebox") into `music/songs/`, add it to `SONGS` in
  `js/ui.js` and its words to `js/lyrics.js` as Gemini sang them (whisper.cpp, large-v3-turbo, `-mc 0`).
- In `updateSound`, play it for its fight the way the Bellwether's song is chosen by enemy id (`strider`, `diver`,
  `yearling`, `oarfish`, `cantor`). The Divers' song takes turns with the score, one fight each, since they are in many
  Belfry fights. Each opens in the Jukebox when its creature is first met, not before (the Dreads' names are not shown
  before then, and the Cantor's song talks about the Heart coming home).
- Check the sung words against WORLD.md, and add the hints that stay (the Yearling hearing a bigger bell through the
  stone, the Oarfish drawn to the glow, "the bell kept every name") to WORLD.md as hints, not facts.
